# Participant information sheet

**Study:** GazeOS, controlling a computer with your eyes · **Contact:** [name, email, phone]

You are invited to try a system that lets people use a computer with their eyes, through an ordinary
webcam. The session takes about 60 minutes, with breaks.

**What you will do:** look at dots on the screen, select targets with your eyes, open a website, type a
few short phrases, read for five minutes, and answer two short questionnaires.

**What we record:** small cropped images of your face and eyes, positions of points on your face, where
dots were on the screen, how long tasks took, and your questionnaire answers. We do **not** keep full
video of you, and your name is replaced by a code (for example P07).

**Risks:** your eyes may feel tired. Tell us any time you want a break or want to stop.

**Your rights:** taking part is voluntary. You can stop at any time without giving a reason, and ask us
to delete your data until [date], after which it is anonymised in the results.

**Use of results:** aggregate results may appear in a project report or paper. No images of you will be
published.

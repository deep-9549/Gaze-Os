# Outreach note to rehabilitation centres and NGOs

Subject: Free webcam-based eye-control software: invitation to try it and give feedback

Dear [name],

We are [names], engineering students at [institution], building GazeOS, a free program that lets people
use a computer with their eyes through an ordinary laptop webcam, with no special hardware. It is meant
for people with ALS, high spinal-cord injury, cerebral palsy and similar conditions who keep good eye
movement.

We would be grateful for a chance to:
- show it to one or two of your therapists for feedback, and
- with consent and ethics approval, invite two or three people who might benefit to try it in a short
  session at a time and place that suits them, with a caregiver present.

There is no cost, no data leaves the computer, and we will share the software with anyone who finds it
useful. Could we have 20 minutes to demonstrate it?
